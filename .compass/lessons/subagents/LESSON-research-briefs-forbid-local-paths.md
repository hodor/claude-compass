Researchers on the whole-overhead axes copied the human's home paths, session ids and a work project name into vault research files; only the pre-commit hook stopped them reaching a commit.
Evidence read from local transcripts carries identifying detail the research does not need.
Brief every researcher up front: cite sources project-free (relative or described), never absolute local paths, session ids or work project names.
