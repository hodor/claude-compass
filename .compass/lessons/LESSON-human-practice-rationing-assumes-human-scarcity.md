The rolling-wave synthesis folded Shape Up's per-wave size budget into its recommended shape on 1-of-3 axis support; the human cut it and banned the framing - size-rationing exists because human throughput is scarce, and AI build speed makes it noise ([[SPEC-015-rolling-wave-planning]] D-03).

When a practice source recommends a cap, budget, or appetite, name the resource it rations and ask whether agents are scarce in it. If not, it is ceremony the human will strip. Carry such an import as a flagged low-support option, never inside the recommendation.
