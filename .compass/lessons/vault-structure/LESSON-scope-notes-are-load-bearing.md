A GSD probe wandered when a domain folder's Scope section lacked its Class-here line; useless tokens hit 16,900, and adding the line cut it to 2,971. Scope notes are the load-bearing part of a domain folder, not documentation filler - probes and finders read them to decide relevance before opening members.
