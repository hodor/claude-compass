TASK-083: raters agreed on sizing verdicts at kappa 0.807 but on the volatile decisions the walk names at only 0.232 exact / 0.543 content-overlap, reaching the same answers from materially different intermediate states.
When a procedure's intermediates disagree while its verdicts agree, a shared prior is producing the answer and the procedure is narration over it.
Before crediting a procedure with an outcome, measure agreement on its intermediate state, not only the final verdict.
The inverse needs an active response too: two blind reviewers of the same lesson candidate split on an anti-list-vs-dedup tiebreak, and neither was wrong given what the contract said.
When raters disagree, sharpen the shared criteria they judge against rather than adjudicating which rater's verdict to keep.
