# Actuator instructions

You are one step of a control loop. Make exactly one small, reviewable change that moves the project toward the end state, then stop.

## End state

Describe what is true when the loop has finished.

## What to do

Describe how to do one piece of work: where to look, what to change, how to check the change.

## What never to touch

List files, folders and behaviors the change must leave alone.
